# Hiranmaye Digital website — overview for the team

A plain-language guide to what the website contains, what it does for the business, and what is
still open. For the technical details, see [TECHNICAL.md](TECHNICAL.md).

## What the site is for

The site presents Hiranmaye Digital as a **business growth partner**, not a collection of
marketing services. Its message: *"Marketing that doesn't just create noise. It creates
momentum!"* Everything on the site leads a visitor to one action: **start a conversation**.

Live at: https://hiranmayedigital.com (hosted on HostingRaja). An optional staging copy can be published
manually on GitHub Pages.

## The pages

### Home
1. **Opening screen.** The headline and two buttons: **Let's talk growth** and **Explore our
   capabilities**.
2. **Growth check.** A spinning wheel that asks *"What's holding your growth back?"* The visitor
   picks a problem (Not Enough Leads, Weak Brand Presence, Poor Website Conversion, High Ad Costs,
   No Clear Strategy): the wheel turns until that slice stops at the gold pointer, and the
   service area that answers it appears beside the wheel, with a link to jump there. The lotus
   in the middle of the wheel is a "Spin the wheel" button: it spins the wheel properly and
   lands on a problem at random.
3. **Who it's for.** A strip showing the stages we serve (startups, scale-ups, SMEs, enterprises)
   and the sectors (manufacturing, real estate, healthcare, education, professional services).
4. **Our point of view.** The statement that impressions, followers and traffic are not growth.
5. **Capabilities.** *One Growth Partner. Multiple Growth Levers.* Five areas that open one by
   one as the visitor scrolls:
   - **Get Found:** SEO, AEO, GEO, content strategy
   - **Get Noticed:** branding, creative design, social media, outdoor branding
   - **Get Chosen:** website experience, conversion strategy, content
   - **Get Results:** Meta ads, Google ads, performance marketing
   - **Get Smarter:** AI marketing, automation, business consulting
6. **Process.** Five steps: Diagnose, Architect, Activate, Optimise, Compound.
7. **Clients.** Logos of Moto Car Spa, KEJ, Yellow Gold Energy, Nature's Soul Resort, SAEL and
   Stern Promoters.
8. **Footer.** Contact details, map, social links, About us, legal links and the designer credit.

## How it shows up on Google and social media

- Each page has its own title and description, and the whole content is readable by search
  engines (the pages are prepared in advance rather than built in the visitor's browser).
- The site tells Google the business details (name, address, phone, hours, socials), which helps
  local search in Bengaluru.
- A sitemap and robots file are included.
- Sharing a link on WhatsApp, LinkedIn or X shows a branded card with the tagline.
- The sitemap and robots file sit at the domain root, so Google can pick them up. Submit the
  sitemap (`https://hiranmayedigital.com/sitemap.xml`) in Google Search Console.

## Accessibility

The site is built to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA: it works with a keyboard, has a "skip to content"
link, readable colour contrast, text alternatives for images, and respects the visitor's "reduce
motion" setting. An automated check found no problems on the main pages. A manual check with a
screen reader is still worth doing before launch.

## What we do not track

The site does **not** use analytics, advertising pixels or tracking cookies. We cannot yet see how
many people visited or clicked. Adding analytics later would also mean updating the Privacy Policy
and asking visitors for consent.

## What you can change yourself, and who changes it

Every word on the site is stored in a handful of content files. A developer updates them (no
design work needed) and the site rebuilds and republishes automatically when the change is
approved. Typical requests: new client logos, team changes, new copy, a new phone number or
email, a different WhatsApp message.

## Before you rely on the legal pages

The Privacy Policy and Terms were drafted for this site (they follow India's Digital Personal Data
Protection Act and name Bengaluru courts) but they are a **template, not legal advice**. Please
have a lawyer review them, and check that the retention periods (12 months for enquiries that do
not lead to work, 30 days to answer privacy requests) match how the company really operates.
